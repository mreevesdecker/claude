What's in this number?
Applied as a flat % to total new product revenue each month — represents the variable cost of delivering the new offerings. The 25% default is a blended estimate; the four products actually have very different cost profiles:
Foundations (one-time, high-touch)~30–40%
LT Transformation (premium MRR)~30–40%
Dept Team B&M (private coach)~20–30%
Dept Leader B&M (self-service)~5–15%
What's included:
  • Coach / contractor labor to deliver programs
  • Coach commissions or revenue-share
  • Onboarding kits & physical materials
  • Platform / software allocation per customer
  • Travel for in-person elements (Foundations)
Not included (lives in operating expenses):
  • Sales commissions or marketing acquisition costs
  • Admin, rent, software subscriptions, executive comp
  • R&D or product development for the suite
